What sits behind the interface

The long-term idea is not one giant model pretending to do everything. NEXUS should route work through the right internal capability and expose one coherent experience to the user.

1 · UserOne simple interface, regardless of what happens behind it.
2 · LevelFree, Smart or Deep defines the expected speed, depth and resources.
3 · NEXUS RouterThe system chooses the best available internal path for the task.
4 · ResultThe user receives one answer or finished piece of work.
